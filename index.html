Core Mechanics

Every shadow hides a threat. Every sound betrays your position. The environment is as hostile as the entities within it.

Next-Gen Atmosphere

Powered by Unreal Engine 5's Lumen, experience suffocating darkness and hyper-realistic dynamic lighting. Your meager light sources are your only lifeline—and a beacon for predators.

Unrelenting AI

The horrors aboard the starship don't just patrol; they hunt. Utilizing advanced navigation and spatial awareness, entities adapt to your routes, flank your positions, and learn your habits.

Physics-Driven Encounters

Interaction is tactile and weighty. Barricade doors with heavy debris, trigger physics-based traps, and manage momentum in zero-gravity segments where every movement counts.
